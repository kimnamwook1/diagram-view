# Privacy

diagram-view runs entirely on your computer. It has no server, collects nothing, and sends nothing to its author or to anyone else.

- **What it reads:** the text of Claude's replies as they are drawn, and the local image and video files those replies name.
- **What it stores:** block sources and rendered pictures in `diagram-view` in the system temp folder. They stay on your computer until your system clears the folder, or you delete it.
- **What it sends:** nothing. Rendering runs offline: see Safety in the [README](README.md).
- **Credentials:** it reads none.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/kimnamwook1/diagram-view/issues.
